## What the numbers say, and what they do not

- **The graph signal itself is clearly stronger.** On its own the vault ranks the evidence better than the frozen graph retriever over the very same nodes: 8 of 19 questions better and 1 worse, nDCG@10 up by 61 percent relative. Large single-question moves: q-cross-05 0 to 1.000, q-single-02 0.237 to 0.613, q-single-03 0.459 to 0.771.
- **That strength does not carry into the hybrid.** The fused arm is slightly worse than the frozen hybrid on average (5 better, 7 worse). The fusion is frozen (equal-weight RRF, D-20), the dense and BM25 lists are the same in every arm, and the control is the best arm, so a stronger graph list helps only where dense and BM25 miss the evidence. This run cannot say more; one slice and 19 questions.
- **Free ablations (exploratory, never gating):** the spread discount is what makes the vault work (`vault` without it: 0.1567, +0.0159 over the frozen graph; hybrid without it 0.3213, -0.0493). Co-mention links add a little (`vault` without them 0.2140; hybrid without them 0.3354). Claims are inert (this vault has none).
- **Semantic resolution (exploratory) did not help:** `vault_sem` 0.2078 against 0.2268 lexical; `hybrid_vault_sem` 0.3315 against 0.3454. The rank-based rule (2 nearest labels, no threshold) was fixed in advance and is reported as it fell.
- **Question-word recognition is the limit the vault does not touch:** 0.3886 of content words recognised (the baseline resolver: 0.5463), and 0.2172 by exact match. Many questions resolve one or two pages. This is a property of the node set the vault is built over.

## Structure of the vault (same slice)

725 pages from 744 nodes (only 16 pages gained an alias: the text defines few abbreviations); 171 asserted and 1,406 co-mention links; 65.5 percent of pages linked (25.4 percent by an asserted link, 60.1 percent by a co-mention); 250 pages with no link; every passage mentioned by at least one page; the top 1 percent of pages own 23.1 percent of mention links (the Mark 1 clause, reported only). The most generic pages mention the most passages ("retrieval" 159 of 285, "Retrieval-Augmented Generation" 126, "generation" 118).

## A qualitative read of 24 of the 100 sampled links (by the assistant; not a score)

The asserted links read well ("SELF-RAG compares_with Critic", "question answering evaluates F1"). The co-mention links are noisier: many come from **cited reference titles that carry no bibliography marker** ("Chain-of-verification reduces hallucination in large language models.", "Tree of clarifications: Answering ambiguous questions with retrieval-augmented large language models"), a few from table rows, and some are sensible coordinations ("critic" and "retrieve" as the two token kinds). The full 100-item sample, with the kind hidden, is for Azim; its key is in `eval/results/audit-keys/`.

## Candidates for a `vault-2` (recorded, NOT applied; each would be a declared amendment and a counted look)

1. Co-mention filter for reference titles that carry no marker (for example a sentence ending in a year, or a passage dominated by citations).
2. A resolver that reads page labels and aliases, not only member node ids, to lift the 0.389 recognition (the largest unaddressed limit).
3. Fusion weight for the graph signal in the hybrid (frozen by D-20; changing it is a different experiment).
4. The semantic step as defined hurt; a different rule would be a new arm, not a tune of this one.

## Limits

One slice, 19 questions, one graph build (g1-3), descriptive only. The gate's clauses 1 to 3 carried no information at this look (D-35 A3). The qualitative read covers 24 of 100 links. Look 3 (an `atomic2` graph, same `vault-1` constants) is the next measurement and is planned after the builder exists.
