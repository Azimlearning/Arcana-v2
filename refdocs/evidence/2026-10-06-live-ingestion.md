# Live ingestion of the benchmark corpus: process and cost record

Generated by `eval/report_ingestion.py` from the spend ledger and the ingestion report. Every number below is
read back from those local files, not estimated. Raw per-call data is in the CSV beside this file.

## What was run

- 16 papers (1,570 chunks of about 1,200 characters, 200 overlap) from the pre-registered manifest, ingested once, one paper at a time, in a data directory used by the benchmark (`data/eval-live-live`).
- Per paper: embed every chunk with OpenAI `text-embedding-3-large` (3,072 dimensions), then one extraction call per chunk with `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` through the LLM seam (provider `anthropic`), schema-free entity and relation extraction, 4 calls in flight, output limit 2,048 tokens (ADR D-25).
- Monthly cap `$10.0`. The ledger reserved each call's worst case before sending it.
