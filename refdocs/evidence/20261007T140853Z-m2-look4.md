## What this look says, and what it does not

- **The new builder makes a better graph, by the one measure that does not depend on the vault.** With the frozen retriever untouched, the graph arm over `atomic2` scores 0.2107 against 0.1408 over g1-3 and 0.1360 over the free-form graph (it recognises more of each question and has far more nodes and edges), at lower cost than g1-3.
- **The vault adds to that on top** (graph arm 0.2871, +0.0765 over the same graph; hybrid +0.0249, now positive where it was negative over g1-3). **Best fused result:** the semantic variant, 0.3624, level with the free-form hybrid (0.3611).
- **Still not better than no graph at this size:** every arm that includes a graph signal is at or below the `flat` control (0.3826) on this 285-passage slice. This is the same sign as Look 3's small corpus, and Look 3 showed it reversing at 1,570 passages with the older graph. **The full-corpus `atomic2` build is the measurement that can show whether this graph, with the vault, beats no graph when the corpus is large** (about $3 to $6.50; needs the cap raised or the 2026-11-01 reset).
- **Not shown:** that any difference here is more than noise (19 questions, one build, no significance test); that the edges are right (the audit sample is unread: 50 stated and 50 co-mention links, kind hidden); that claims help beyond +0.007.

## Limits

One slice, 19 questions, one build per graph (no temperature, so a rebuild would differ), descriptive only; the gate's clause 3 is uninformative; the sem arms and ablations are exploratory; the g1-3 and `atomic2` graphs differ in the prompt, the evidence window and the claims at once, so the gain cannot be attributed to one of them.
