# P2 D2: the admission-rate study (the first measurement of the bound)

Run `20261006T184643Z-bound`, results in `eval/results/20261006T184643Z-bound.json`. Code: commit
`c906c4d5e68dac8c562fcf1dc2410d0fd6841793`, `dirty: false`. The protocol is the docstring of `eval/bound.py`, fixed and committed
before this run; nothing about it was changed afterwards.

**What it is.** For each of the frozen 20 questions and two layers, which catalog components' preconditions the evidence
satisfies, using the catalog's own `check` functions and the caps in the templates' fences, with the reason code for
each refusal. `flat` is the dense and BM25 retrievers fused; `hybrid` adds graph retrieval and the Graph agent's deterministic
tools (path or neighbourhood, and `align_documents`), run on every question. **What it is not:** a quality score, a
proof of the paper's claim, or anything about questions unlike these 20. It is an admission rate over 20 questions on one
ingestion. The answer component (`CitedSummary`) needs a Research answer, which the study does not produce, so its
admission is a proxy (retrieval returned a passage) and is not a finding.

**Run conditions.** Stores `data/eval-live-live`, with the hashes checked against the one-shot ingestion's: graph
`00454fd3...f830`, vectors `3cd9426d...8f0e`, chunks `20fd0ba6...ddfb` (all equal to the expected values passed on the
command line). Providers live for embeddings only. 20 query embeddings were computed (shared by the layers). The
ledger went from $6.109429 to $6.109495: **a delta of $0.000066, 20 new entries, all `embed`**. No model call was made.

## What the numbers say, and what they do not

1. **The bound is measurable and the graph extends it.** The set of components flat retrieval can ever admit is two; the
   hybrid layer realised all five. That the extra three need the graph is by construction (their preconditions read
   entities and relations); the measurement is how often those are available: a connection graph for 80 percent of
   the questions, a document comparison for all, a multi-hop path for 15 percent.
2. **The preconditions are about evidence, not about the question being answerable.** The control `q-single-01` (the
   question the corpus cannot answer, kept as a "should find nothing" control) was **not** refused the relational
   components: it admitted a `GraphView` (4 drawable edges), a `ComparisonGrid` (6 papers x 4 entities) and a `ConceptMap`
   (4 hops), because the entities named in its text (its words resolved to 5 graph nodes) do have grounded relations in the
   graph. The protocol had written that the control's expected admission of relational components is none; the
   measurement says otherwise, and that expectation was wrong. The right reading is that admission means "the evidence
   needed to draw this exists", and that the real turn's answer component, not the structural preconditions, is what
   says "nothing found" for this question. A relevance gate on the relational components is a design change that this
   study motivates and does not make; it is not made after seeing the result.
3. **The comparison grid is available for every question and is not selective.** It was admitted on 20 of 20, with the
   6-paper cap reached every time and 4 to 8 entity columns. The question's own entities come first and shared neighbours
   fill the rest, which on this corpus include generic hubs (see D1 for how it looks on screen). Admitted does not mean useful.
4. **The multi-hop path is rare because every hop must be grounded.** 15 of 20 refusals are `ungrounded_hop`: some
   hop on the shortest path had no stored sentence naming both of its concepts. This is the strict rule working, and it is
   the dominant cost of that component (B2 measured 44 percent of the Graph agent's edges as drawable).
5. **Four questions get no connection graph** (`q-cross-06`, `q-cross-12`, `q-single-03`, `q-contra-04`): none of the
   edges the agent returned had a quote naming both endpoints.

## Limits

20 questions on one ingestion (16 papers, one extraction model); the questions are not a random sample and share papers; the
resolution hit rate (0.65) bounds what the graph signal can supply; the answer component is a proxy; the Graph agent was run
on every question although the Orchestrator would not route every one to it, so this is availability and not routing;
nothing here says a component, once admitted, is correct or helpful.
