# P3-lite: does a pretrained cross-encoder re-ranker help the hybrid arm? (D-37)

Result: `eval/results/20261014T135007Z-p3-rerank.json`. Tool: `eval/rerank_compare.py`; its protocol (arms, depths, headline pairs, decision rule) was committed **before** this run (commit `2617eae`; the run's own commit is recorded in the result, clean tree). Corpus, questions and gold: the P1 benchmark's (16 papers, 19 answerable questions); the stores' graph, vector and chunk hashes equal the baseline's, and the recomputed `hybrid` top ten equals the baseline's for every question (checked by the tool, which refuses otherwise). **Spend: $0.000062** (19 question embeddings; ceiling $0.01).

Engine: `Xenova/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` (a pretrained cross-encoder, not trained here) through `fastembed` (ONNX, CPU), behind `api/engines` (additive: absent, failing or malformed, the RRF order stands; a test pins each case).

## Result (19 questions, mean over questions)

| Arm | nDCG@10 | Recall@10 | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hybrid` (frozen) | 0.208 | 0.212 | per-signal top 10, RRF k = 60 |
| `hybrid_n30` | 0.238 | 0.305 | the same arm with 30 per signal and after fusion, scored on the top ten (depth control) |
| `hybrid_n50` | 0.231 | 0.292 | the same with 50 |
| `rerank_n10` | 0.211 | 0.212 | the frozen ten, re-ordered (cannot change recall) |
| `rerank_n30` | 0.260 | 0.282 | the top 30 of `hybrid_n30`, re-ordered by the engine |
| `rerank_n50` | 0.224 | 0.239 | the top 50 of `hybrid_n50`, re-ordered |

Mean candidate recall (the share of gold passages anywhere among the N candidates): N = 10: 0.21, N = 30: 0.42, N = 50: 0.50. Two questions (`q-cross-12`, `q-contra-02`) have no gold passage in the top 50 at all.

## The verdict, by the rule fixed in advance

**Headline pair, `rerank_n50` against `hybrid_n50`, nDCG@10: mean difference -0.007, 7 questions better, 7 worse, 5 tied, exact Wilcoxon p = 0.90. The engine is reported as NOT SHOWN TO HELP.** The other named headline pair (`rerank_n50` against the frozen `hybrid`) is +0.016, 7 / 7 / 5, p = 0.95: also no evidence of a gain, and it conflates the re-ranker with the deeper candidate list.

## What the numbers do say

- **Candidate depth matters more than ordering.** With 30 per signal before fusion, the hybrid arm's own Recall@10 rises from 0.212 to 0.305 and nDCG@10 from 0.208 to 0.238, with no model involved (`hybrid_n50` against `hybrid`: Recall@10 +0.080, 8 better, 4 worse, p = 0.21; exploratory, not significant on 19 questions). The frozen protocol cuts each signal at ten; a depth of 30 is a candidate protocol amendment for the final benchmark, and it would be declared before that run, not applied here.
- **The re-ranker's effect is small and mixed in sign** (+0.021 at 30 candidates, -0.007 at 50; never significant) and it does **not** improve with more candidates, which suggests the general-domain MS MARCO model transfers poorly to PDF-extracted retrieval-paper passages (long chunks are truncated at 512 tokens, and the text carries PDF noise).
- **It is slow for what it gives:** 50 passages take about 5.0 s on this machine's CPU (mean over questions, maximum 8.4 s), against a v1 budget of 200 ms added.

## Limits

19 questions, one candidate generator, one pretrained model, one machine; a non-significant difference is not evidence of no effect. The oracle ceiling computed from the stored results before the run (0.208 to 0.290 for re-ordering the same ten) already said the gain available by re-ordering is small. A domain-adapted or larger cross-encoder, or truncating chunks to their best sentences, might do better and was not tried; none of that would be a trained-by-us model.

## What this decides

D-37 stands as confirmed in the form "one engine, measured": the engine is built, evaluated against its heuristic with the fallback verified, and **left off by default** because it was not shown to help and costs seconds. The distilled router and a trained mastery model remain unbuilt, for the reasons in the deliberation. If Objective 03 asks for a trained model evaluated against its heuristic, this is that evidence, including a negative result.
